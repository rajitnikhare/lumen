What Lumen does and doesn't do

Encrypted at rest
Document text, notes, history, reminders, drafts, research, imported mail, and your API keys use an encrypted vault. There is no passphrase recovery.
Explicit memory
Only documents, notes, and imported mail with AI access enabled enter retrieval. Preferences are saved only when you create and authorize them.
No silent actions
Search queries and deletions require single-use approvals. Lumen cannot send emails, read calendars, or connect messaging accounts. Mail import is read-only and only happens when you click Check for new mail.
Device-bound availability
The computer must be on. The vault auto-locks after 30 minutes without an action. Keep FileVault enabled.
Honest limits
Models can be wrong. Keyword and optional semantic retrieval can miss evidence, citations need verification, research compares snippets rather than full articles, and suggested reminders can miss or misread dates in your mail. This does not protect against malware running as you.
